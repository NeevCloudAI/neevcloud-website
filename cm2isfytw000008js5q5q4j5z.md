## What is an AI SuperCluster?

An **AI SuperCluster** is a highly interconnected, powerful computing environment that combines **AI-optimized hardware**, advanced software platforms, and high-speed networking to facilitate large-scale AI training and deployment. These superclusters, often hosted in **AI Clouds** or **AI SuperClouds**, are designed to handle massive datasets, complex algorithms, and real-time processing.

AI SuperClusters typically include:

* **Thousands of GPUs or TPUs** operating in parallel.
    
* **High-performance networking fabric** that ensures low-latency communication between nodes.
    
* **Optimized AI frameworks** like TensorFlow, PyTorch, or JAX running in unison.
    
* **Massive storage arrays** for training datasets and AI model checkpoints.
