## The Cost Conundrum: Buying vs. Renting

Buying GPUs can feel like a bad first date: high upfront costs with ongoing maintenance and surprise expenses lurking around the corner. Take the NVIDIA A100, priced around $11,000. Or the cutting-edge [H200](https://www.neevcloud.com/nvidia-h200.php), which can set you back a whopping $40,000. Sure, they’re powerful, but you’ll need a financial safety net just to bring one home!

Now, let’s look at renting. Picture this: you could access these same GPUs for as little as $2 to $5 per hour on cloud platforms. You get the muscle without the commitment—kind of like dating a gym rat who doesn’t ask for a ring!

## Why Renting Might Be Your Best Bet

**Flexibility at Its Finest**  
Imagine your AI project changes direction faster than a politician at a debate. In the world of AI, project requirements can change overnight. Renting allows you to adapt quickly. One minute you’re training a model on an [H100](https://www.neevcloud.com/nvidia-h100.php), the next you’re back to your trusty laptop because the boss decided to pivot. No long-term commitments, just pure flexibility!

**Stay Ahead with Cutting-Edge Technology**  
With tech evolving faster than a toddler with a new toy, renting ensures you always have access to the latest GPUs. Want to try the new [B200](https://www.neevcloud.com/nvidia-b200.php) architecture? It’s like the latest iPhone launch—if you’re stuck with an older version, you might feel a bit left out! Rent it for a month, and if it doesn’t work out, you’re not left with a high-priced paperweight. Breakups are easier when it’s a rental!

**Mitigating Financial Risk**  
Investing in hardware can feel like trying to predict the stock market—exciting but risky. Renting means you only pay for what you use, eliminating that heart-stopping moment when you realize you’ve just invested in outdated tech. No one wants to be that person clinging to a V100 while everyone else is buzzing about the A100!
