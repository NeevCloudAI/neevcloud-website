### What Is the Blackwell Platform?

The **NVIDIA Blackwell platform** is the latest architectural evolution in NVIDIA’s GPU lineup, succeeding the Hopper and Ampere generations. Named after David Blackwell, a trailblazer in statistics and mathematics, this platform integrates cutting-edge hardware design principles to address the most demanding AI workloads.

#### Key Features of the Blackwell Platform:

* **High Throughput:** Designed to handle complex AI models with unprecedented speed and efficiency.
    
* **Energy Optimization:** Implements next-generation power management to reduce environmental impact.
    
* **Modular Scalability:** Built to scale seamlessly across cloud-based GPU deployments in AI datacenters.

### NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 Design Contributions: Open Hardware Innovation

One of the standout elements of the Blackwell initiative is NVIDIA's decision to contribute the **GB200 NVL72 design** to the open hardware ecosystem. This step ensures greater accessibility for researchers and developers and fosters innovation across industries.

#### Highlights of the GB200 NVL72 Design:

* **High-Performance Memory Access:** Supports ultra-fast communication between processing units, critical for AI model training.
    
* **Optimized for Large-Scale Models:** Tailored for generative AI and large language models (LLMs).
    
* **Open Source Impact:** Encourages collaboration, allowing developers worldwide to build on NVIDIA's foundation.
