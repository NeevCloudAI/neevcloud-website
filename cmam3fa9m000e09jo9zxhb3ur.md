## 3\. Blackwell Architecture: What’s New?

The RTX 5090 is built on NVIDIA’s Blackwell architecture, a major step forward from previous generations. Here’s what makes it special:

### Enhanced CUDA and Tensor Cores

* **CUDA Cores:** The sheer number of CUDA cores (over 18,000) means more simultaneous calculations, translating to faster results in parallelizable tasks.
    
* **Tensor Cores:** The 5th-generation Tensor Cores are specifically designed to accelerate AI and machine learning operations, supporting new data types and improved efficiency.
    

### GDDR7 Memory

* **Speed and Capacity:** GDDR7 memory offers higher speeds and larger capacities than ever before, crucial for handling massive datasets and complex models.
    
* **Bandwidth:** With up to 1.7 TB/s bandwidth, bottlenecks are virtually eliminated, ensuring smooth data flow even in the most demanding scenarios.
    

### Ray Tracing and AI Enhancements

* **3rd-Gen RT Cores:** These enable real-time ray tracing at greater fidelity, making the RTX 5090 ideal for scientific visualization, architectural rendering, and VFX.
    
* **AI-Driven Features:** Improved support for AI-powered denoising, upscaling, and other advanced graphics techniques.
    

### Power Efficiency

* **Cloud Optimization:** Despite its power, the RTX 5090 is designed for efficient operation in cloud data centers, with optimized power consumption and thermal management.
