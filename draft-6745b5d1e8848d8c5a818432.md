## What is the NVIDIA Quadro RTX8000?

The RTX8000 is part of NVIDIA's Quadro RTX series, designed specifically for professionals requiring robust graphical and computational performance. Built on NVIDIA's Turing architecture, the RTX8000 is tailored for tasks such as AI model training, real-time ray tracing, and large-scale simulation.

### Key Specifications:

* **CUDA Cores:** 4,608
    
* **Tensor Cores:** 576 (ideal for AI and deep learning workloads)
    
* **VRAM:** 48 GB GDDR6 (expandable up to 96 GB with NVLink)
    
* **Ray Tracing Cores:** 72
    
* **Memory Bandwidth:** 672 GB/s
    
* **FP16, FP32, and FP64 Support:** Flexibility for diverse workloads
    
* **Display Outputs:** Up to 4 4K displays or 8K HDR
