### 3\. Key Features of the NVIDIA H100

The NVIDIA H100 GPU comes packed with new and enhanced features tailored to handle high-scale AI, data analytics, and high-performance computing (HPC) needs. Here’s a breakdown of the features:

* **Hopper Architecture**: Tailored specifically for AI applications, delivering unprecedented speed and performance.
    
* **Transformers Engine**: The NVIDIA H100 boasts a dedicated engine to optimize transformer models, improving natural language processing tasks.
    
* **4th Gen Tensor Cores**: With support for FP8 precision, the H100 allows for faster computation while maintaining model accuracy.
    
* **Multi-Instance GPU (MIG)**: Enables partitioning of the H100 for multiple simultaneous applications or users, maximizing GPU utility.
    
* **NVLink**: Enables high-bandwidth, low-latency connections between GPUs, allowing for seamless scalability in multi-GPU environments.
    
* **DPX Instructions**: Speeds up graph analytics and dynamic programming, ideal for real-time AI applications.
    
* **PCIe Gen 5**: With faster data throughput, the H100 supports faster model deployment and data processing.
    

### 4\. NVIDIA HGX H100 System Overview

The NVIDIA HGX H100 is a turnkey solution for enterprises looking to leverage multiple H100 GPUs in a unified system. Designed specifically for high-performance data centers, the HGX H100 integrates multiple GPUs, making it ideal for training large AI models or handling diverse workloads.

* **Composed of up to 8 NVIDIA H100 GPUs** for a powerhouse of computational performance.
    
* **Enhanced NVLink Bandwidth**: Ensures each GPU can work in tandem with minimal data latency.
    
* **Perfect for AI, Machine Learning, and HPC applications** in data centers aiming for optimal processing power.

### 6\. Pricing Details: H100 GPU and HGX H100 System

The H100’s pricing structure reflects its advanced capabilities, making it a premium choice for businesses ready to invest in high-performance AI solutions. While exact pricing may vary depending on configuration and vendor, here is a general overview of what enterprises can expect:

* **NVIDIA H100 GPU Price**: The standalone H100 GPU is estimated to be between $30,000 and $40,000. This price can vary based on customization and additional features.
    
* **NVIDIA HGX H100 System Price**: The HGX H100, equipped with up to 8 H100 GPUs, ranges significantly depending on the specific setup. Enterprises may expect prices from $200,000 to $400,000 or more for this powerful setup.
    

**Factors Affecting Price**:

* Number of GPUs (e.g., a standalone H100 vs. an HGX setup with 4-8 GPUs)
    
* Additional cooling or specialized components for data center deployments
    
* Service and support packages
