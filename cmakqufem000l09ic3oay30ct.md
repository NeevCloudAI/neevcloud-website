## FAQs

### What is a GPU-enabled cloud, and why is it important for AI growth?

A GPU-enabled cloud provides on-demand access to Graphics Processing Units (GPUs) through cloud platforms, allowing businesses to process compute-intensive AI workloads faster and more efficiently than CPUs. It enables scalable AI training, reduces model development time, and supports large-scale deep learning applications.

### How does GPU cloud infrastructure reduce AI model training time?

By using parallel processing, multiple GPUs can train large AI models simultaneously. For example, training a large language model (LLM) on 64 NVIDIA GPUs can reduce weeks of computation to hours. Pipeline optimization and hardware tuning further enhance performance.

### What are the benefits of GPU-as-a-Service (GPUaaS) for businesses?

GPUaaS offers cost efficiency through pay-per-use models, rapid scalability for varying workloads, global accessibility for distributed teams, simplified infrastructure management that lets teams focus on AI model development, and robust compliance and security features for sensitive industries.
