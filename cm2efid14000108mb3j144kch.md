## **What is the NVIDIA H200 GPU?**

The **NVIDIA H200** is the latest addition to NVIDIA’s Hopper architecture lineup. It is specifically designed to meet the increasing demands of large language models (LLMs), generative AI, high-performance computing (HPC), and deep learning applications. The H200 delivers breakthrough performance in parallel processing and data-intensive tasks, making it a key enabler for the [**AI cloud**](https://www.neevcloud.com/) ecosystem.

With its **advanced tensor cores** and **next-generation NVLink**, the H200 bridges the gap between hardware and software, allowing developers to accelerate model training, inference, and real-time analytics more efficiently than ever.

## **Key Features of NVIDIA H200**

1. **Hopper Architecture Enhancements**  
    The **H200** continues the innovation introduced by NVIDIA’s Hopper architecture. It supports a new range of tensor core operations optimized for large-scale AI models. Compared to the previous **H100**, the H200 offers improved computational power per watt, resulting in greater efficiency.
    
2. **Increased Memory Bandwidth**  
    Memory bandwidth plays a crucial role in training large AI models. The H200 boasts **up to 5TB/s of memory bandwidth** when used with NVIDIA’s **HBM3e memory**, ensuring smooth data transfers across GPU memory and minimizing latency for complex calculations.
    
3. **NVLink 5.0 Support**  
    The H200 supports **NVLink 5.0**, which enables faster inter-GPU communication. This capability allows multiple GPUs to operate seamlessly within a distributed AI cloud environment, making it ideal for tasks like multi-node model training.
    
4. **FP8 and Dynamic Precision Modes**  
    The H200 introduces **FP8 (8-bit floating-point precision)** alongside traditional FP16 and FP32 modes, reducing memory requirements for AI workloads without compromising accuracy. This feature plays a crucial role in deploying large language models efficiently within the AI cloud.
    
5. **Green AI & Energy Efficiency**  
    In the era of sustainability, the H200 prioritizes **energy-efficient AI** by offering **30% better performance-per-watt** compared to its predecessor, the H100. This makes it a valuable asset for organizations aiming to reduce operational costs and carbon footprints.
