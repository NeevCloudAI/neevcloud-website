## **Statistical Insights on AI Advancement Driving Cloud Adoption**

The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence (AI) is significantly influencing cloud adoption across various sectors. Here are key statistics, facts, and figures that illustrate this trend:

### **Economic Impact of Cloud and AI**

Global GDP Contribution: In 2023, cloud computing contributed over $1 trillion to the global GDP, with cloud-enabled AI generating approximately $98 billion. Projections indicate that by 2030, the combined contribution of cloud computing and AI could surpass $12 trillion in global GDP

### **Regional Contributions:**

* **U.S. and Canada:** $457 billion from cloud computing, with $58 billion from cloud-enabled AI.
    
* **Europe:** $321 billion from cloud, with $30 billion from AI.
    
* **Asia Pacific:** $152 billion from cloud, with $7 billion from AI
    

### **Cloud Adoption Rates**

* **Enterprise Usage:** Over 94% of organizations with more than 1,000 employees utilize cloud services. Notably, about 48% plan to migrate at least half of their applications to the cloud within the next year
    
* **Hybrid Cloud Strategy:** More than half of organizations are adopting a hybrid cloud approach, while the use of multiple public clouds is rapidly increasing, rising from 7% in 2022 to 14% in 2024
    

### **AI Integration in Business**

* **AI Adoption by Large Firms:** More than 50% of companies with over 5,000 employees leverage AI capabilities. This figure rises to over 60% for firms with more than 10,000 employees
    
* **Investment in AI:** A survey indicated that 96% of customers link business AI adoption directly to migrating operations to the cloud. Furthermore, over half (52%) plan to increase their AI budgets by 25% to 50% in the next two years
    

### **Spending Projections**

* **Cloud Services Growth:** End-user spending on Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) is projected to grow by 26.6%, while Platform as a Service (PaaS) spending is expected to rise by 21.5%, and Software as a Service (SaaS) by 15.9% in 2024
    
* **Market Size Forecasts:** The public cloud market is expected to reach approximately $825 billion by 2025, growing at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 21.3%, up from $561 billion in 2023
